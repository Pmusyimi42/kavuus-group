About Kavuus Group

Kavuus Group is a member-driven savings and welfare group founded on the principles of unity, trust and shared responsibility. We bring members together through regular contributions, collective support and a commitment to building a stronger financial future.

How Kavuus Group Works

Monthly Contributions

Each member contributes KSh 2,000 every month towards the group's savings pool.

Four-Month Collection Cycle

At the end of each four-month cycle, the selected member receives the agreed dispatch amount. Any remaining collections are retained as group reserves, subject to the group's financial rules and expenses.

Member Dispatch

One member receives KSh 80,000 during each four-month cycle, following the agreed rotation.

Continuous Participation

Members continue making their monthly contributions even after receiving their dispatch, allowing the group to support the next member.

Membership

Membership at Kavuus Group is open to individuals who share our vision of unity, financial discipline and mutual support. Prospective members are required to submit an application, which must be supported by an existing group member. All applications are reviewed by the group leadership, and membership is confirmed only after approval.

The group leadership is responsible for reviewing membership applications and making the final admission decision. Successful applicants are formally welcomed into Kavuus Group and granted access to member services and group activities.
